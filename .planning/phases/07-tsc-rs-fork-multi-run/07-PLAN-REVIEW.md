2. **07-02 T3 (3b) — the fresh-leg recycle assert fails on snippets 2–5; the bench gate exits 1.** `data?.held?.instanceGen === 6 && data.held.recycles === 5` runs once per snippet, but `heldGen` accumulates for the isolate's lifetime and all 5 snippets share one `TSCHECKER_WASM_RECYCLE_AFTER:1` boot: snippet k ends at gen 6·k with recycles 6·k−1 (gen 12/18/24/30). Only slugify-iter1 passes. **Fix:** make the expectation cumulative, e.g. `const expectedGen = (SNIPPETS.indexOf(snippet) + 1) * 6;` then assert `data?.held?.instanceGen === expectedGen && data.held.runsOnInstance === 1 && data.held.recycles === expectedGen - 1` (label: "recycled every run"), and update the AC's "gen-6/recycles-5 asserts" wording to match (the per-run fresh semantics remain fully asserted via `runsOnInstance === 1`).
